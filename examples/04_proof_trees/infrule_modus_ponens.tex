\noindent Modus ponens is a fundamental inference rule in propositional logic. If we know p is true and p implies q, we can conclude q is true.

\bigskip

\begin{infrule}
  p & \\
  p \implies q & \\
  \derive \\
  q & \mbox{modus ponens}
\end{infrule}

\noindent The horizontal line (generated by ---) separates premises from conclusion. The label in brackets appears to the right of the conclusion.

\bigskip

\noindent Here's a more complex example with multiple premises:

\bigskip

\begin{infrule}
  p \implies q & \\
  q \implies r & \\
  p & \\
  \derive \\
  r & \mbox{chain of implications}
\end{infrule}

\noindent We can also use INFRULE without labels:

\bigskip

\begin{infrule}
  A \land B & \\
  \derive \\
  A & \mbox{conjunction elimination}
\end{infrule}
